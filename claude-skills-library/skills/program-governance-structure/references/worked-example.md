# Worked example: GIC Gili route launch governance (small-scale, right-sized)

| Forum | Purpose | Membership | Cadence | Decision authority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly check-in | Track progress, unblock day-to-day issues | Owner, ops lead | Weekly | Operational decisions within existing budget |
| Go/no-go review | Decide whether to proceed to public launch | Owner (sole decision-maker for a business this size) | Once, after scouting + unit economics complete | Launch decision itself |

### Escalation triggers
| Trigger | Escalates to |
|---|---|
| Any change requiring budget beyond the originally agreed scouting budget | Owner, immediately (not held for the weekly check-in) |
| Any safety-related finding from the scouting trip | Owner, immediately |

### Decision log
| Date | Decision | Made by | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| [date] | Proceed with scouting trip | Owner | Needed before any launch decision can be made responsibly |
| [date] | Delay public marketing pending dock resolution | Owner | Scouting trip found the first-choice dock unviable at low tide (see RAID log) |

For a two-person operation, this is deliberately lightweight — one
weekly check-in and one go/no-go review, not a multi-layered committee
structure a much larger program would need. The discipline that matters
here is the escalation triggers and the decision log, not forum count.
