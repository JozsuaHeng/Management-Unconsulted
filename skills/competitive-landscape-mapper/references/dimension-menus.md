# Comparison dimension menus by industry

Pick the 4-6 dimensions most relevant to the actual decision — don't use
every dimension listed here for every comparison.

## Tourism / charter / hospitality
Offering (route, duration, inclusions), price point, positioning/target
customer, fleet or room count (scale), review rating + volume, booking
channel presence, seasonality of operation.

## Professional services / consulting
Service scope, pricing model (fixed/hourly/retainer), target client size,
industry specialization, team size, notable clients or case studies
(only if publicly disclosed).

## Retail / e-commerce
Product range, price tier, channel (online/physical/marketplace),
shipping/fulfillment model, brand positioning, customer review profile.

## General-purpose default (when industry isn't listed above)
Offering, price point, positioning, scale, reputation/reviews — this is
the default set used in the main SKILL.md output format.

## A note on verification discipline

When public sources conflict (e.g. one review site says 3 boats, the
company's own site says 5), state both and flag the discrepancy rather
than picking one arbitrarily. When a competitor's ownership structure
isn't publicly clear (e.g. franchise-style operations, or multiple
brands sharing infrastructure), say "ownership unclear" rather than
guessing — an unverified claim stated as fact is worse than an honest gap.
