# Worked example: readiness check, 1 week before booking system go-live

| Group | Capability | Sentiment | Communication reach | Leadership support | Overall |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Booking staff | Medium — trained, but one person missed the session | Medium — one team member openly worried about slowing down during peak season | High — both aware and have used the test environment | High — owner visibly involved in training | Medium |

### Gaps requiring action before go-live
| Gap | Action | Owner | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| One staff member missed training | Schedule a 1:1 catch-up session | Owner | 3 days before go-live |
| Concern about slower bookings during peak season | Address directly: agree a temporary "buddy" system for the first two weeks so a second person is available during peak hours | Owner | Before go-live |

### Evidence basis
Based on a direct conversation with both booking staff members (high
confidence) — sentiment score reflects an actual stated concern, not a
guess. Leadership support score is self-assessed by the owner and not
independently verified, which is a reasonable limitation for a
two-person team.
