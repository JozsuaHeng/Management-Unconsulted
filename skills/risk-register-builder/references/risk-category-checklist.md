# Risk category checklist

Use this to brainstorm broadly before scoring anything.

- **Operational** — staffing, equipment/maintenance, capacity, supply
  chain, process failure points
- **Financial** — cash flow, cost overruns, currency exposure, payment/
  collection risk
- **Reputational** — customer experience failures, negative reviews,
  public incidents, social media exposure
- **Regulatory/legal** — licensing, permits, contract disputes,
  compliance changes
- **Market/external** — competitor moves, demand shifts, seasonality,
  weather, macroeconomic factors

## Worked example: launching a new multi-day Gili route (risk register
excerpt)

| Risk | Category | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crew unfamiliar with Gili-area navigation/mooring | Operational | Medium | High | Scouting trip + crew training before first paying guests | Ops lead |
| Weather/monsoon disrupts scheduled crossings | Market/external | Medium | Medium | Build flexible rebooking policy, communicate seasonality clearly upfront | Booking lead |
| No established local dock relationship | Operational | High | Medium | Secure at least one confirmed dock partnership before public launch | Owner |
| Negative first-guest reviews during early operational learning curve | Reputational | Medium | High | Soft-launch with a small number of trips before full public marketing | Owner |

### Top priorities
Crew navigation familiarity and lack of dock relationships are the two
highest-priority items — both are high-likelihood-or-impact and both are
addressable before public launch, unlike weather (which is accepted and
managed via policy rather than eliminated).

### Accepted risks (example)
General weather variability is accepted as an ongoing operational reality
of the route, managed through flexible rebooking rather than an attempt
to eliminate it.
