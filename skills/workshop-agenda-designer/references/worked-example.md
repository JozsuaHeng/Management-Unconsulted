# Worked example: GIC annual strategic planning workshop

Required output by end of session: agreed priorities for the next 12
months, each with an owner and a rough timeline (feeding into
`plan-on-a-page`).

| Time | Duration | Section | Activity | Produces |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9:00 | 15 min | Opening | Frame the session's required output; quick round of "one thing going well, one thing you're worried about" | Shared context, everyone's spoken once early |
| 9:15 | 45 min | Current state | Individually write 3 honest current-state observations, then share and cluster as a group | A shared, non-spin view of where things stand |
| 10:00 | 15 min | Break | — | — |
| 10:15 | 60 min | Priority generation | Small-group brainstorm (2 groups of 2) on "what would most move the business forward this year," then cross-share | A long-list of candidate priorities |
| 11:15 | 15 min | Break | — | — |
| 11:30 | 45 min | Prioritization | Apply `impact-effort-prioritizer` live to the long-list as a group | A short-list of 3-5 real priorities |
| 12:15 | 30 min | Ownership & close | Assign an owner and rough timeline to each priority; confirm next check-in date | The actual required output |

### Facilitation notes
- Opening round: keep it to one sentence each — this is about getting
  everyone's voice in the room early, not a full discussion yet.
- Current state section: do the individual-write step before any group
  discussion — this prevents the most senior or loudest voice from
  anchoring everyone else's view of "how things are going."
- If the brainstorm stalls, prompt with "what's the thing we keep
  saying we should do but haven't" — usually unlocks real answers.

### Output capture
One person (not the facilitator, who should stay focused on running the
session) takes notes throughout, using the agenda's "Produces" column as
a structure — the final artifact is the priority list with owners,
written up and circulated within 2 business days while it's still fresh.
