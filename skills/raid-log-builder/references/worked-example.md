# Worked example: GIC Gili route launch RAID log (excerpt)

### Risks
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weather disrupts scheduled crossings | Medium | Medium | Ops lead | Open |

### Assumptions
| Assumption | If wrong, impact is... | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| A local dock partnership can be secured before launch | Launch delayed, or a costlier alternative dock needed | Owner | Unverified |
| Fuel costs stay within current budget assumptions | Route pricing/margin needs revisiting | Owner | Unverified |

### Issues
| Issue | Impact | Owner | Status | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scouting trip revealed the first-choice dock isn't viable at low tide | Delays confirming route logistics | Ops lead | In progress | 2 weeks |

### Dependencies
| Dependency | Needed from | Needed by | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crew training/scouting completion | Ops team | Before public marketing starts | Ops lead | In progress |

Note the dock issue: it started life as a risk ("dock might not be
viable"), and once the scouting trip confirmed the problem, it moved
into Issues with a resolution target — this is the reclassification
discipline the skill calls out, not a new item created from scratch.
