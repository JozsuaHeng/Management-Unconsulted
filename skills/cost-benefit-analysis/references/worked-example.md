# Worked example: illustrative booking-platform investment case

Time horizon: 3 years, figures approximate/illustrative.

### Costs
| Cost | One-off / recurring | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Platform license | Recurring (annual) | $X/year |
| Setup and data migration | One-off | $Y |
| Staff training time (2 people, 1 day each) | One-off | ~$Z (opportunity cost) |

### Benefits — quantified
| Benefit | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Reduced booking-error rework | Estimated 3 hrs/week saved × hourly rate × 52 weeks | $A/year |
| Fewer missed/duplicate bookings | Estimated 1% of annual booking revenue currently lost to errors | $B/year |

### Benefits — qualitative (real, not monetized)
- Reduced staff frustration and burnout risk during peak season
- Better data for future decisions (accurate booking history, not a
  spreadsheet prone to overwrite errors)

### Net position
Quantified benefits ($A+$B/year) minus recurring cost, over 3 years,
minus one-off setup — payback expected within [N] months based on the
quantified benefits alone, before qualitative benefits are even
considered.

### Sensitivity
Most sensitive to the estimated 3 hrs/week rework-time saving — this is
an estimate, not a measured fact. If the real saving is closer to 1
hour/week, payback extends meaningfully but the qualitative benefits
(staff stress reduction, data quality) still support the case
independently.
