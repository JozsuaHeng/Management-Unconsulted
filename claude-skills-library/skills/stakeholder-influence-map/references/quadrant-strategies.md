# Quadrant strategies in detail

## High influence, high interest — Manage closely
Actively involve in decisions, not just updates. Give them a genuine
voice, seek their input before major moves, and address concerns
directly. These are the people who can single-handedly make or break the
initiative — under-investing here is the highest-risk mistake on the
grid.

## High influence, low interest — Keep satisfied
They could block or slow things if annoyed, but don't need or want
detailed involvement. Give periodic, high-level updates; avoid
over-communicating (which can itself create unwanted interest/scrutiny);
make sure they're not surprised by major changes even if they're not
actively engaged day to day.

## Low influence, high interest — Keep informed
They care and may have useful input or be affected directly, but can't
block or significantly accelerate the initiative on their own. Give them
real visibility — regular updates, a way to ask questions — since their
goodwill matters even without formal power, and disengaging them
entirely can create resentment or lost insight.

## Low influence, low interest — Monitor
Minimal proactive effort. Include in broad communications but don't
invest special engagement time. Re-check periodically in case their
influence or interest changes (e.g. a promotion, or the initiative
becoming more visible to their area).

## Worked example: introducing a new booking system at GIC

- **Owner/decision-maker (you)** — High influence, high interest → Manage
  closely (this is the driving stakeholder, not one being "managed" in
  the traditional sense, but still worth mapping to keep the plan honest
  about where the decision authority sits)
- **Booking/inquiry team member** — High influence (day-to-day system
  user, adoption depends on them), high interest (directly affects their
  daily work) → Manage closely — involve them in choosing/testing the
  system, not just informing them once decided
- **Boat captains/crew leads** — Medium-low influence, medium interest
  (affects scheduling visibility but not their core job) → Keep informed
- **Existing booking platform/agent partners** — Variable influence
  depending on how much business they route, likely low-medium interest
  unless the change affects how they submit bookings → Keep satisfied or
  informed depending on actual integration impact — worth confirming
  directly rather than assuming
