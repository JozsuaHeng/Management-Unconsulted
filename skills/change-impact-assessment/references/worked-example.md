# Worked example: GIC booking system replacement

Change: replacing the shared spreadsheet booking process with a
dedicated booking platform.

| Group | Process impact | Systems impact | People/role impact | Culture impact | Magnitude |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Booking/inquiry staff | Full change — availability checked and bookings entered in a new system, not a spreadsheet | New platform to learn; old spreadsheet retired | None — same role, new tool | Some initial frustration expected during learning curve | Large |
| Ops/crew scheduling | Minor — still receives a schedule, just generated from a different source | Views schedule in new platform instead of shared file | None | Minimal | Small |
| Accounting | Deposit/payment data now flows from the new platform instead of manual entry | New integration point to understand | None | Minimal, though errors during transition need a fallback plan | Medium |
| Customers | Booking confirmation process may look slightly different (new email template, possibly a booking portal) | None directly | None | Minimal — mostly invisible to them if done well | Small |

### Groups needing the most support
Booking/inquiry staff — full process and tool change, direct daily
impact. This is where training and hands-on support should concentrate;
other groups need only light communication.

### Confidence
Based on a direct conversation with the two people who currently handle
inquiries (high confidence on their impact); accounting and customer
impact are based on planning assumptions and should be checked once the
platform's integration details are confirmed.
