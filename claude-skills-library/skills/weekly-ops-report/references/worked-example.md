# Worked example

Raw input (illustrative): "This week we had 8 bookings, up from 5 last
week. One cancellation due to weather. Crew training day happened
Tuesday. Still waiting on the dock contact reply from last week. Nothing
major broke. Next week planning to finalize the scouting trip date."

### Output

**Headline**: A solid week — bookings picked up and crew training
happened as planned, though the Gili dock contact is still an open
follow-up.

### Key numbers
| Metric | This week | Last week | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bookings | 8 | 5 | +3 |
| Cancellations | 1 (weather) | — | — |

### What happened
- Crew training day held Tuesday
- One weather-related cancellation

### Issues / blockers
- Still waiting on reply from the Gili dock contact (outstanding since
  last week — worth a follow-up nudge)

### Coming up next week
- Finalize scouting trip date

Note what this version does *not* do: it doesn't invent extra detail
about the crew training content (not provided), and it explicitly
surfaces the outstanding dock contact as a blocker rather than letting it
sit inside a generic bullet.
