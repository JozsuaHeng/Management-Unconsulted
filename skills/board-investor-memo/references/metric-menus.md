# Metric menus by business type

Choose metrics that actually matter to the specific business — a generic
metric list is a sign the memo wasn't tailored.

## Charter / tourism / hospitality
Bookings (count and revenue), occupancy/utilization rate, average booking
value, cancellation rate, review rating and volume, repeat-customer rate,
lead time (booking to trip date).

## Professional services / consulting
Active engagements, revenue (billed and pipeline), utilization rate
(billable hours), client retention/renewal rate, new business pipeline
value.

## General SaaS/subscription (for reference if relevant to a client)
MRR/ARR, churn rate, net revenue retention, customer acquisition cost,
active user count.

## Tone notes for risk sections

Investors and boards generally respond better to a risk stated plainly
with a mitigation plan than to a risk buried in cautious language or
omitted. Compare:

Weak: "There were some challenges with staffing this quarter that we are
monitoring."

Better: "Inquiry response time slowed after a team member's departure in
August; we're cross-training a second person on inquiry handling,
targeting response time back under 2 hours by [date]."

The second version names the actual problem, its cause, and a concrete
fix with a timeline — that's what makes a risk section useful rather than
just an acknowledgment.
