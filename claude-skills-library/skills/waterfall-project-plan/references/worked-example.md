# Worked example: illustrative, generic regulatory reporting system build

| Phase | Deliverable | Owner | Gate criteria | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Requirements | Signed-off requirements document | Business analyst | Regulator's mandatory fields confirmed and signed off by compliance | 3 weeks |
| Design | Approved technical design | Solution architect | Design reviewed against every signed-off requirement, no open questions | 4 weeks |
| Build | Working system in test environment | Dev lead | All designed functionality implemented, unit tested | 8 weeks |
| Test | Signed-off test results | QA lead | All acceptance criteria pass, no unresolved Sev-1/2 defects | 3 weeks |
| Deploy | Live in production | Dev lead | Compliance sign-off on go-live readiness | 1 week |

### Change control
Any proposed change after Requirements sign-off is logged, impact-
assessed against schedule/budget/regulatory risk by the project manager
and business analyst jointly, and requires Executive approval before
being incorporated — with the schedule/budget re-baselined if approved,
not silently absorbed.

This is exactly the kind of project Waterfall genuinely fits: regulatory
requirements are externally fixed and well understood upfront, and
formal sign-off at each phase is often a compliance necessity, not just
a process preference.
